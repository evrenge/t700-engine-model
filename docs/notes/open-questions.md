| 2 | What exactly is the "opened" compressor mass-flow iteration, and how does it carry state across frames? | Phase 4 integration scheme | pdf pp.36-37 read from 600-dpi crops, equation by equation; pdf p.38 for the consequence; pdf p.47 for the rates actually used | **closed** — mechanism found on pdf p.36. Under the quasi-steady approximation P3 and P41 are solved algebraically each frame, and two nested loops appear: an **inner** loop coupling P3 and P41 across the combustor (Eq. 76 gives P3 from P41; Eq. 78 gives P41 from P3), and an **outer** loop through the compressor map, `P3 → Ps3 = K_Ps3·P3 → WA2_c = f₁(Ps3/P2, NG_c) → WA2 → {WA3, WA3_bl} → WA31 = WA3 − WA3_bl → P3`. Closing the outer loop would mean re-evaluating the compressor map several times per frame — the most expensive computation in the model. **"Opening" it means cutting that loop and running it exactly once per time step, with the combustor inlet flow built from a compressor mass flow carried over from the previous frame:** `WA31_(n) ≈ WA3_(n) − WA3_bl(n−1)` (Eq. 74, pdf p.36), the report's own words being "the outer loop was separated from the inner loop by assuming that the mass flow entering the combustor for a given interval is approximately equal to the mass flow leaving the compressor in the previous interval". **State carried across frames:** one mass flow, lb_m/sec — an extra saved scalar beyond the integrator states. **Initialization is not stated** (see the row on initializing the cross-frame mass flow, #23). **Why time-step dependent:** a converged closed loop would give the same answer for any Δt (algebraic loops have no memory); the opened loop replaces convergence with a one-frame delay, leaving a residual of order (dWA3/dt)·Δt — pdf p.37, "the approximation results in a value of iteration error which is a function of the time step". **The 0.1% criterion:** three separate statements, do not conflate — (a) pdf p.37, inner P3/P41 loop, "up to ten iterations … with less than 0.1 percent error, resulting in a total of 110 arithmetic operations"; (b) pdf p.37, P45 loop, "Eight iterations resulted in an error equal to less than 0.1 percent of the steady-state value"; (c) pdf p.38, the opened outer loop, "a restriction on time step to a maximum value of 10 msec was established based on a maximum allowable error of 0.1 percent between time steps". Only (c) sets Δt; it is a **relative, frame-to-frame** error, but the report never says on which variable (see the row on the 0.1% criterion, #24).
