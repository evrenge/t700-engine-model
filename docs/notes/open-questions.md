what is missing, where it is needed, where it was looked for, and what was decided. A closed
row carries its resolution and the evidence that settles it; the investigation behind it is in
`git log -p docs/notes/open-questions.md`. A number in a row is as measured on the date the row
gives unless it points to `site/assets/report.json`.
