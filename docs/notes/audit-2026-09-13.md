## Already fixed

### `run()` logged the pressures one frame behind the speeds — `db5bad3`

In the quasi-steady scheme P3/P41/P45 are algebraic: solved *inside* a frame from the
state at `t_i`, and returned in the next state. `realtime.run` recorded them **before**
calling `step`, so every trace paired this frame's NG and NP with the previous frame's
pressures. Verified exactly: `p3_before[i+1] == p3_after[i]` to 0.0.

Invisible at steady state, **12.1 psia on P3** at the Figure 9 step. It flattered a
headline number: the Figure 9 phase-plane agreement is **1.42 %**, not the 0.59 %
reported. Figure 10 moved 1.07 → 1.02 %. The whole-curve RMS is unmoved (one frame in
715), which is why no test caught it.

That *sharpens* the conclusion rather than weakening it. Figure 9's phase-plane panel was
already known to be nearly vacuous — a chord through its two endpoint trims fits Ballin's
own trace to 0.30 % — and at 1.42 % our deviation is now larger than the curvature that
panel would have to explain, so it carries nothing. Figure 10 remains the informative one.

## Engine-physics audit — all six closed, 2026-09-13

1. **The inner P3/P41 loop stops on the iterate step, not the error.** Measured
   contraction ρ = 0.884–0.910, so the remaining error is ≈8–11× the last step.
   `TOL_PRESSURE = 1e-3` therefore delivers ~1 %, not the "less than 0.1 percent error"
   its docstring claims [pdf p.37]. Through the Figure 9 step: P3 −1.41 %, NG −0.19 %,
   T41 peak −18.7 °R against the same loop run to its true fixed point. Worst frame exits
   at the 10-pass cap **6.5 % from its own fixed point**. Tightening the tolerance
   recovers nearly all of it; raising the cap recovers almost none. **New datum for open
   question #27: our Lipschitz constant is L ≈ 0.91.**
2. **The 2:1 multirate is declared and not implemented.** `FRAME_NP_S = 0.014` is
   referenced nowhere in `src/`; `MAX_STEP_S = 0.010` is never enforced. Measured cost:
   ≤14.9 rpm (0.068 %) on NP over 2 s. Structural/documentation gap, not numerical.
3. **Non-convergence is computed and discarded.** `inner_iters`/`p45_iters` reach
   `FrameOut` and `run()` drops them, so a run spending frames at the cap emits no signal.
   `maps.clamp_report()` does the right thing for the other failure mode.
4. **Map extrapolation at the operating point, counted but never surfaced.** `f6` is
   clamped at *every* trim above ~590 lbm/hr (FAR 0.0327 against a domain ending at
   0.0200) — numerically harmless, 1e-4 on f6, but outside the data over the top third of
   the power range. `f1` crosses the right-hand end of the lower bracketing speed line
   during the Figure 9 acceleration — that end is the digitized surge boundary — biasing
   WA2c up to +0.33 %, and `TrimResult.on_data` does not test for it.
5. **Sign-blind combustor flow.** `wa31 = sqrt(max(dp,0))` plus `far = 0` when `wa31 ≤ 0`
   injects fuel and releases no heat if P41 ≥ P3. Latent — 0 occurrences across every
   trim from 100 to 800 lbm/hr and both transients — but it is a silent substitution.
6. **`maps._clamps` is module-global mutable state inside the model core**, mutated by
   every map lookup, i.e. inside `engine.frame`. CLAUDE.md excepts only the thermo
   backend. Determinism holds; `frame()` is impure and clamp accounting is non-reentrant.

**Quantified for the first time, no defect:** Eq. 29's non-conserving station-4.5 mix
destroys **51.0 Btu/s = 1.35 % of fuel heat = 72 hp** at hover and puts T45 23 °R below a
mass-weighted mix. A conserving mix needs K_H45 = 0.9765; the report prints 0.9623, and
0.9623 is what reproduces Table B.1's T45.

**Verified clean:** all three volume balances close to ≤2e-13 lbm/s; combustor energy to
1e-12 Btu/s; Eq. 39 is *exactly* energy-conserving across the compressor; every equation
dimensionally balanced; 16 Jacobian sign checks correct; δ-similarity exact to 0.000e+00;
heat-sink realization right, DC gain exactly 1, k ∈ [0.594, 0.685]; the quasi-steady fixed
point equals the differential trim to machine precision; no unphysical state anywhere in
100–800 lbm/hr (η_c 0.80–0.82, η_gt 0.78–0.80, η_pt 0.83–0.88, entropy rises across both
turbines, P3 > P41 > P45 > P49 > Ps9 throughout).

## Numerical-mathematics audit — all ten closed, 2026-09-13

1. **Eq. 80's P45 iteration is divergent below ~76 %NG, and this overturns a recorded
   claim.** `g(P45) = N/f9(Ps9/P45)` has `g'(P45*) = dln f9/dln(Ps9/P45)`. **Verified
   independently:** f9's elasticity is −0.25 / −0.76 / −0.95 / −1.26 / −1.57 at
   Ps9/P45 = 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.75 / 0.80 / 0.849, so the fixed point is **repelling** above
   ≈0.77 — i.e. below roughly 165 lbm/hr. `_p45_loop` has no divergence test and returns
   whatever it holds at the cap.

   The agent reports the iterate 2-cycles so the answer depends on the **parity** of the
   pass count (even caps → 75.745 %NG, odd ≥3 → 67.598, against a differential trim of
   67.039). **My own check did not reproduce the parity table** — every cap I tried gave
   75.74 — but my check monkeypatched `realtime.MAX_ITER_P45`, which is almost certainly
   bound as a default argument at definition time and so had no effect. **The parity claim
   is therefore unverified, not refuted; the repelling-fixed-point mechanism is verified.**

   Either way, two recorded claims are wrong about the *mechanism*:
   `test_the_printed_p45_tolerance_admits_a_false_equilibrium_below_flight_idle` says "it
   is the P45 tolerance, not the pass cap" and evidences it with caps 8 and 20 — both
   even. And open question #45's explanation — "a loose tolerance on a clamped, therefore
   nearly flat, iteration function" — is backwards: at that root Ps9/P45 = 0.849 is
   *inside* f9's domain, f9 is not clamped, and the map is expansive rather than flat.
   Also reported: tightening the **inner P3/P41** loop alone removes the false root just
   as well, so the attribution to P45 is not established.

2. **Three more sub-idle equilibria, none pinned.** At 150 lbm/hr the printed-tolerance
   run never settles — ±200 rpm after 171 400 frames. At 140 and 130 lbm/hr the settled
   speed is 4.98 % and 2.00 % off the differential trim **at both tolerances**, so these
   are genuine second roots of the frame map, not convergence artifacts.
3. **`logm(A_d)` is not real at the printed tolerance.** At trim 3, `A_d` has a negative
   real eigenvalue (z = −0.0828), so `np.real(logm(A))` discards ±π/dt = ±448.8 rad/s and
   `‖expm(Re logm A) − A‖/‖A‖ = 0.189`. The eigenvalues survive by a structural accident
   (a simple real eigenvalue has a real spectral projector), so `test_discrete_map`'s
   numbers are right — but the operation is invalid in general and nothing guards it.
   Adjacent: that file indexes the sorted spectrum positionally (`[-2]`, `[-3]`), which is
   exactly what `test_linear.py::test_sorting_would_mispair_the_slow_modes` forbids.
4. **`SCOPE.md:163` claims the Jacobian uses the report's ±2 % perturbation. The code uses
   1e-5**, and ±2 % would move the spectrum by **21.6 % / 2.6 % / 22.7 %**.
   `test_eigenvalues_are_step_independent` sweeps 1e-4…1e-8 — three decades away from the
   step it claims to use.
5. **The "2e-8 central-difference floor" is a value at one step, not a floor.** The
   minimum over step size is ~4e-9, 5–6× smaller. Quoted in #49 and in
   `test_discrete_map`'s docstring.
6. **`SpeedMap` turns a NaN parameter into the top speed line, silently and uncounted**:
   `f1(5.0, nan)` → 10.108 (the 100 % value) with an empty clamp report, because
   `nan < lo or nan > hi` is False and `searchsorted` returns `len(params)`. A
   NaN-corrupted speed yields *maximum* compressor flow instead of a detectable NaN.
7. **`SpeedMap`'s docstring claim that the interpolation order matters is false** —
   resampling onto the union of knots and blending gives an identical result to 3.6e-15.
   The real consequence of unequal breakpoints is the f1 clamp in finding 8.
8. **f1 has a 15-point hole between the 65 % and 80 % speed lines**, and where the 65 line
   is clamped the map slope collapses to `(NGc−65)/15` times the 80-line slope — 0.067 at
   NGc 66, 0.333 at 70. The ∂/∂NGc gradient jumps 2.16× across the 80 line. **This band is
   exactly where the Figure 10 chop and the 110–175 lbm/hr trims live**, i.e. where
   findings 1 and 2 appear.
9. **`trustworthy` only tests `f1:parameter`.** At 110 lbm/hr three tables are clamped at
   the solution and `trustworthy` is True. `f9` clamped is precisely the ingredient #45
   names, and it is not checked.
10. **Six control lags run at dt/τ = 0.7** (CTPL, TL1, TL2, T17, CT9, CTPS3 at τ = 0.010 s
    against dt = 0.007). Effective τ is 41.9 % short; at dt = 10 ms — `MAX_STEP_S` — they
    go **deadbeat**, and at 14 ms the pole is negative and they stop being lags at all.
    **Measured consequence at the frame actually used is small** (closed-loop settled
    state moves ≤0.045 % from dt 7 ms → 0.875 ms; T41 slam peak 0.23 %), because every
    affected block is a sensor lag far above loop bandwidth. Nothing records the condition.

**Verified clean:** Euler stability margins are 51×–400× on every physical mode;
accumulated error is *zero* (a trimmed engine holds 120 s at 0.00 ppm; the post-step
settled state is identical to 11 significant figures across dt = 14 → 0.4375 ms; repeated
runs are bit-identical); no catastrophic cancellation bites (worst 1.55 digits on
P3 − P41; the "thirteenfold Ps3 leverage" is confirmed at 9.2–9.4 analytic and measured);
Newton in `trim.solve` is sound (cond 5.5e3–2.2e5, divergence handled, the scaled-absolute
criterion is the right choice); Appendix B row 6 is genuinely ill-conditioned as printed
(±50 of rounding moves the printed slow mode over [−0.565, +7.191]) while *our* row 6 is
25 000× better conditioned; `_blocks.delay` reproduces 0.015 s exactly at every dt tried.

## Dependency and packaging audit — all nine closed, 2026-09-13

1. **A non-editable install is broken.** `maps.py` and `appendix_b.py` resolve data as
   `__file__.parent.parent.parent/"data"`, which only exists in the source tree; no
   `package-data` is declared and `data/` lives outside `src/`. `pip install .` then
   `maps.f1()` → `FileNotFoundError`. Editable installs and pytest's `pythonpath` mask it.
2. **The sdist ships the tests but not the data they read.** 50 entries, none matching
   `data/`, `tools/`, `validation/`, `docs/`. No `MANIFEST.in`.
3. **`tools/digitize_a1.py` rewrites a committed data file on *import*** — it is the only
   tool with no `__main__` guard. `python3 -c "import digitize_a1"` regenerates
   `f1_compressor_mass_flow.csv`.
4. **The reproducibility gate cannot run from a fresh clone.** `digitize_a1.py` hardcodes
   `validation/out/digitize/a1r_600-056.png`, which is gitignored and which no tool in
   `reproduce_all.sh` produces. It fails loudly rather than falsely passing, but CLAUDE.md
   names that gate as one of two `live` mechanisms and it is unenforceable elsewhere.
5. **`.[dev]` alone cannot run the suite** — `testpaths` includes `validation/`, which
   imports scipy at module level, and scipy is in `tools`. Collection dies, `tests/`
   included.
6. **Holes in `tests/test_imports.py`.** The AST walk is better than expected (it catches
   function-level, `TYPE_CHECKING` and try/except imports), but these escape:
   `importlib.import_module("scipy")`, `ctypes.CDLL`, `timeit.default_timer`, `os.times`,
   and — because numpy is the one allowed package — **`np.random.default_rng()`**, which
   voids the RNG ban outright. Also `"os.urandom"` in `NONDETERMINISTIC` can never match
   (only top-level names are yielded), and the `constants.py` exemption is directory-blind
   so `control/constants.py` is silently exempt too. **No live violation exists** — `src/`
   is clean on every vector — these are holes in the mechanism.
7. **Nothing lets anyone reproduce the environment.** No container definition, lock file,
   or recorded Python version; every dependency is a bare lower bound. Live drift: numpy
   2.4.6 (container) vs 2.5.3 (fresh install), scipy 1.16.2 vs 1.18.1, pytest 8.4.2 vs
   9.1.1. Verified *not* breaking: the suite passes on both and the trim is bit-identical
   across numpy versions. **poppler is an undeclared external prerequisite** —
   `pdfimages`/`pdftoppm` are invoked by the digitizers and the string "poppler" appears
   nowhere in the repo.
8. **The source PDF's own provenance is unrecorded** — no NTRS accession, no URL, no
   checksum, and its embedded metadata is empty. Every citation is `pdf p.NN` against
   *this specific scan*. SHA-256 is
   `55af702919aa809871fcc2035f063d22388ca8d797ae18c7879ac0e4c7cc82e7`.
9. **The public-domain claim is over-broad in one checkable way.** The report's Figure 2
   is captioned "(From ref. 6.)", and reference 6 [pdf p.102] is a **General Electric**
   training guide, not a government work. Ref. 4 is a Sikorsky report. Separately,
   17 U.S.C. §105 removes copyright *in the United States* only.

**Verified clean:** declared-vs-used is correct in both directions; the pillow/control
correction is complete and right; `requires-python = ">=3.11"` is correct and tight
(`StrEnum` is the binding constraint); no numpy-2-removed API anywhere; all subprocess
calls are list-form with `shell=False`; no network at import; no vendored third-party
code; all 66 tracked CSVs cite only TM-100991.
